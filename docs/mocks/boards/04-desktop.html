Camera Buddy roadmap: the best Sony companion app

Plan18 min readv5 by Claude, 2 h ago

Where we stand

Camera Buddy already ships four tools: a full Bluetooth remote, Wi-Fi live view, Wi-Fi import, and card culling with XMP. No single competitor combines all four.

What "best" means

Connects the first time and stays connected: QR join, a connection doctor that detects the known failure causes, and work that survives leaving the app.

Pro shooting tools: exposure control, peaking, zebras, false colour, histogram, time-lapse with ramping, bracketing and focus stacking.

The market is wide open: the a6400's only Sony app is rated 1.6★ from 6.7K ratings, and Sony's culling app shut down in June.
