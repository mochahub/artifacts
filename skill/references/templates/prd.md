# Template: prd

What to build and why, for people to agree on.

**Use it for:** Before building a feature.

Start with `artifacts init --template prd --slug <slug>`. Everything it copies is yours to change: the subject, the screens and the structure are your call, and you can skip any of this.

- Start with the person and the problem, before any solution.
- Write down what's out of scope, and why.
- Make goals measurable, including the number that mustn't get worse.
- End with the specific decision you need, and by when.
- Put mocks on a canvas page in the same artifact, and embed frames with `::frame`.

Replace the `summary` in `artifact.json` before publishing, and see `../theme.md` for colours and fonts.
