# Template: case-study

What happened, told so someone else can learn from it.

**Use it for:** After a project or launch, for customers, or to share a lesson.

Start with `artifacts init --template case-study --slug <slug>`. Everything it copies is yours to change: the subject, the screens and the structure are your call, and you can skip any of this.

- Put the result first: on the cover, and in the first sentence.
- Use `:::stats` for the outcome, `:::steps` for what was tried, and `:::compare` for before and after.
- Keep what went wrong. It's why people believe the rest.
- Replace `00%` on the cover in `boards/cover.html`.

Replace the `summary` in `artifact.json` before publishing, and see `../theme.md` for colours and fonts.
