# Template: research-report

An answer to a question, with how sure you are.

**Use it for:** Investigating a question before a decision.

Start with `artifacts init --template research-report --slug <slug>`. Everything it copies is yours to change: the subject, the screens and the structure are your call, and you can skip any of this.

- Put the question on the cover, and lead with the answer.
- Write findings as claims, marked `{Verified}` or `{Unverified}`.
- Say what you couldn't find.
- End with a recommendation someone could act on.
- For several reports, make each a page with `"group": "Research"`.

Replace the `summary` in `artifact.json` before publishing, and see `../theme.md` for colours and fonts.
