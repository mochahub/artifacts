# Template: weekly-status

Where things stand, in a minute's reading.

**Use it for:** A regular update for a team or stakeholders.

Start with `artifacts init --template weekly-status --slug <slug>`. Everything it copies is yours to change: the subject, the screens and the structure are your call, and you can skip any of this.

- Lead with the one thing that mattered, and whether anyone needs to act.
- Done, in progress, blocked, next. Cut any heading with nothing under it.
- A blocker names who can unstick it.
- Publish each week as a new version of one artifact, so people can compare weeks.
- Update the week and the counts on the cover in `boards/cover.html`.

Replace the `summary` in `artifact.json` before publishing, and see `../theme.md` for colours and fonts.
