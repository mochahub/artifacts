# Writing articles

- Lead with the answer. Say what you found before how you found it.
- Use short, plain sentences. Prefer concrete numbers to adjectives.
- Say how sure you are: `{Verified}` for what you ran or read, `{Unverified}` for what you didn't.
- One idea per paragraph, and headings a reader can skim.
- Use a table to compare things, and a list for real sequences. Don't turn every sentence into a bullet.
- Stable headings keep comments attached, so don't reword them without a reason.
